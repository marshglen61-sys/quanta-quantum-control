One game. Three principles.

QUANTA is built around a small set of interacting ideas. Every move changes the balance of control across the board.

Clusters & layers

Particles form Clusters. The top contiguous same-colour group is the Dominant Layer and controls the Cluster.

Shift or slide

Each turn gives you two Moves. Shift a Particle or Moving Group, or Slide a neutral Boundary.

Singularities

Control at least three Singularities, declare QUANTA and try to hold your position until your next turn.
